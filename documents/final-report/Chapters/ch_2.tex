\section{Clinical Background}

Chronic respiratory diseases account for more than 4 million deaths globally per year \cite{}.
Research shows that early detection of pulmonary diseases, particularly lung cancer and Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
(COPD) dramatically lowers mortality rates.
Two landmark studies, the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST) and the Nederlands-Leuvens Longkanker Screenings Onderzoek (NELSON)
trial, have shown that low-dose CT (LDCT) screening educes lung cancer mortality by 20\% to 33\% compared to CXR \cite{nlst2011, nelson2020}.
